## About This Repo

Getting hackathon sponsors is one of the hardest parts of organizing an event. This repo gives you:

- A curated list of **200+ companies** that sponsor hackathons
- **Direct links** to sponsor application pages, credits programs, and DevRel contact info
- **Organizer guides** from MLH, Devfolio, Devpost, and Hack Club
- A **copy-paste email template** to cold-reach any company

> **Note:** Sponsorship decisions depend on event size, location, audience, and timing. Always send a clear proposal and follow up politely. Most companies have limited budgets — apply 2–3 months before your event.

## How to Reach Out

### Step 1 — Find the Right Person

Search LinkedIn for these job titles at your target company:
- **Developer Relations** / **DevRel**
- **Developer Advocate**
- **Community Manager**
- **Startup Programs Manager**
- **University Recruiter / Technical Recruiter** (for hiring-focused sponsors)

**LinkedIn Search:** [linkedin.com/search — Developer Advocate](https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/people/?keywords=developer+advocate)

### Step 2 — Build a Tracking Spreadsheet

| Column | Why |
|---|---|
| Company | Name |
| Contact Name | From LinkedIn |
| Title | DevRel / Community / HR |
| Email | Found or guessed (firstname@company.com) |
| Status | Not contacted / Sent / Follow-up / Confirmed |
| Follow-up Date | 3 days after first email |

### Step 3 — Send the Intro (Short & Specific)

Include:
- Hackathon name + date
- Expected number of participants
- What you want: cash / credits / swag / mentors
- What they get: logo on site, booth, hiring access, talks

### Step 4 — Follow Up

- **First follow-up:** 2–3 days after initial email
- **Final follow-up:** 6–7 days after initial email (mention it's your last follow-up)
- **If no reply:** Move on — it's not personal. Volume matters.

### Tips That Actually Work

- **Target local companies first** — they sponsor for talent recruiting nearby
- **Ask for in-kind when cash is unavailable** — API credits, swag, and subscriptions are easier approvals
- **Lead with specifics** — "500 developers, 48 hours, Oct 12–13 in Bangalore" is better than "a big hackathon"
- **Mention your prize track** — "We'd love a dedicated Groq prize track worth $300" gets a faster yes
- **Apply 2–3 months early** — most DevRel budgets have quarterly planning cycles

## Email Template

Copy-paste this to cold-reach any company. Customize the placeholders.

```
Subject: Sponsorship Opportunity — {HackathonName} ({Date}, {City})

Hi {Name},

I'm {YourName}, organizing {HackathonName} — a {Duration}-hour hackathon on {Date} in {City} with {Number}+ participants from {Universities}.

We'd love to partner with {Company} as {SponsorshipTier} sponsor. Here's what we're looking for:
- {CashAmount OR "API credits / swag / licenses"}

What {Company} gets:
- Logo placement on website, socials, and event banner
- Dedicated booth / table at the event
- Direct access to {Number} student developers (hiring pipeline)
- {SpecificPerk, e.g., "a {Company}-themed prize track"}

Can I send over our sponsorship deck?

Best,
{YourName}
{YourEmail} | {LinkedIn}
```
